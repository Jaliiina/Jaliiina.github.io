About

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of International Business and Economics (UIBE), where I rank first in my major. My research focuses on building reliable learning and reasoning systems for complex, noisy, and heterogeneous data.

I am particularly interested in graph machine learning, LLM-based agents, and data-driven decision systems. My work spans robust graph representation learning, financial and enterprise text analytics, retrieval-augmented generation, and biomedical data analysis.

I aim to develop AI systems that can retrieve evidence, integrate multiple data sources, and produce reliable outputs for analysis and decision-making.
